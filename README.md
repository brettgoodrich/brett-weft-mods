# weft-gold-armor
Standalone Weft mod that adjusts gold armor defense and damage bonuses.

"Glass Cannon" for gold.
At full gold armor, 3x damage. Otherwise, 20% more damage per piece.
Gold armor's durability and defense is minimal. Each piece only grants 5% damage reduction.

| Gold pieces worn | Incoming damage reduction | Melee damage multiplier |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 0% | 1.0x |
| 1 | 5% | 1.3x |
| 2 | 10% | 1.6x |
| 3 | 15% | 1.9x |
| 4 | 20% | 3.0x |
